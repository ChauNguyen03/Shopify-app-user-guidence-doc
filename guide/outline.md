**"How to use" sub-sections** (page numbers keep counting straight through, they don't restart per sub-section):
- **Product pages** — the main product page and its header/footer
- **Product detail** — editing individual product data shown on cards
- **Other pages** — page types built like Product pages, but not about products: Cover, Back, Custom pages, and Table of contents
- **Custom section** — the blocks (Text, Shape & line, Image, Button, QR code, Product card) and the Arrange/position/size controls shared by all of them, used across every page type
