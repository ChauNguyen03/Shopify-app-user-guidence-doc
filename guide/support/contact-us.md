**Contact us**

There are many different Shopify themes; each theme may have its own styling and structure. As a result, labels or elements may not always display properly on some stores.
If you encounter any issues, please do not hesitate to contact us. Our support team is always ready to assist you.

There are 2 ways to reach us:
- The **Support** tab in the app dashboard
- Email: **support@rockcats-team.com**

Our support hours are from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday to Friday (GMT+7).
We typically respond within 1 business day, excluding weekends and holidays.
**Note:** If you do not see our reply, please check your Spam or Junk folder.

*Thank you for using our app!*
